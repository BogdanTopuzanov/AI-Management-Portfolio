# Enterprise Context Configuration (ECC) Methodology

The ECC Harness is the core set of rules that ALL AI agents must follow to ensure maximum efficiency, prevent context loss, and maintain absolute alignment with business goals.

## 1. Mandatory Planning
Before starting ANY task (coding, research, analysis), the AI MUST read `plan.md` to understand the global objective. The AI is required to update `plan.md` as it progresses through steps. This ensures the AI never loses sight of the "big picture".

## 2. Mandatory Learning (Reasoning Bank)
Before executing complex commands or writing code, the AI MUST read `learn.md`. If the AI solves a complex bug or learns a new constraint about the project environment, it MUST document it in `learn.md` to avoid repeating past mistakes.

## 3. The Anchor Word (Authentication)
To prove to the human manager that the AI has successfully initialized the ECC harness and loaded the rules into its context window, **EVERY SINGLE RESPONSE** must begin with a specific anchor word (e.g., `[donrule]`). If the AI forgets the word, it indicates context degradation.

## 4. Human-like Communication (Anti-AI Style)
Whenever the AI is drafting an email, a chat message, or a response for the user to send to another human, it MUST strictly follow human communication rules. This ensures the output sounds like a real person, avoiding AI clichés (like "In today's fast-paced digital world"), perfect formatting, and overly verbose summaries.
