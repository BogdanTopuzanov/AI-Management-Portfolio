# Mass Job Parser (LinkedIn & Indeed)

## Overview
A highly efficient, AI-assisted scraping tool designed to parse job vacancies from LinkedIn and Indeed at scale. The system leverages local AI models to extract key information (skills, salary, requirements) from unstructured job descriptions and store them in a structured database.

## Role of AI Management
- Orchestrated the data extraction pipeline.
- Wrote fallback mechanisms for captchas and IP bans.
- Used local LLMs for parsing raw HTML/Text into JSON schemas.
